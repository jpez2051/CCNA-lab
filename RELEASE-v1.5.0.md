# CCNA Launchpad v1.5.0 — teach, apply, explain

The beginner starting check previously directed five correct answers toward switching. That was not evidence that a learner understood the fundamentals. Every starting-check result now leads to the first lesson; the dashboard resumes the last explicitly opened stage, never an automatically inferred proficiency level.

## Delivered

- Optional starting checks include “I do not know yet” and a confidence/explanation self-check. Results show the original question, selected answer, correct answer and explanation, with written correct/incorrect labels. A correct answer never marks mastery.
- All ten stages have three ordered teaching steps, worked reasoning, glossary access, associated practice and fresh short application scenarios. Scenario decisions and ungraded written reasoning are stored separately from reading completion.
- A VLAN-path practical adds observable state, configurable faults, healthy cases, bidirectional modeled traffic tests and verification after the latest change. Advanced models vary faults, including healthy and multiple-fault cases. Route tables include nonmatching more-specific prefixes and no-route cases.
- Associated reading completion stays in the lesson. Routing opens OSPF, switching opens VLAN practice, security opens ACL practice, and DHCP infrastructure opens the relay guide.
- The revised 100-item rehearsal uses versioned IDs and revised options. Legacy forms remain readable for saved sessions, but are no longer offered as new pilots. Untimed teaching scenarios use a separate pool. Earlier source-item exposure is explicitly carried into the revised rehearsal; revised wording is not treated as unseen content.
- Missed and unanswered exam questions appear first. A filter isolates them. The current form plus ten prior completed forms can be reviewed and exported.
- Notebook projects have separate names and notes, preserving the original notebook. Progress backups preserve projects and attempt history.
- Scenario review intervals grow from 2 to 7 to 21 days for successful unassisted attempts on separate days. Mistakes or help shorten review to one day. No streak or mastery score is introduced.
- Footer, title, asset URLs and displayed application version are v1.5.0.

## Review and limitations

This is a software and authored-content improvement, not a declaration of complete CCNA preparation. Multiple-choice decisions still do not grade free-text reasoning. Short scenario variants can become familiar. Larger real-world builds, IPv6 depth, wireless, FHRP, AAA, VPN, monitoring and automation still require additional instruction and practical work.

External Packet Tracer recipes remain unexecuted on a named simulator version. No `.pkt` starter or reference topology is claimed. Independently reviewed exam forms, calibrated difficulty, learner pilots and pass prediction remain pending. Rewording earlier questions does not create an independent held-out exam. Firebase/cloud work remains deferred.

Validation covers legacy progress and CLI rules, new normalization and migrations, numerical answer keys, form scoring and exposure, spaced-review scheduling, and desktop/mobile browser flows. The browser suite checks all-correct and all-unknown starting checks, lesson/lab continuity, scenario feedback, VLAN verification, no-route handling, project reloads, exam resume/expiry, missed-answer filtering and older lesson lab sequencing.
