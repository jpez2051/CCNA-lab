# v1.3.0 — Connected learning lab

Implemented a ten-stage entry path from physical devices through addressing, routing, services, troubleshooting and introductory CCNA domains. Each stage links to a practical or tool and keeps the learner in the lesson after marking reading complete. Existing reading, lab and question records are retained.

Added a limited observable IPv4 sandbox with predictions, tests, targeted repairs, fresh verification after changes, and before/after evidence. Guided and state-revealing attempts are distinguished from independent fault attempts. This is an explicit teaching model, not an IOS or packet emulator.

Added a binary/subnet tool (/0–/32), installed-route lookup trainer, six downloadable Packet Tracer build sheets including a separate practical capstone, structured evidence notebook, backups/restoration, spaced revisit prompts, and original held-out pilot forms A/B (20 items each). Exam answers persist through navigation/reload; the deadline continues, feedback waits for submission/expiry, and repeats are labeled exposed practice.

Expanded foundation scenarios with local/remote destinations, cached/uncached ARP, additional troubleshooting evidence, shuffled options and retained per-field answers/checks.

## Critical review and limits

- The beginner sequence is improved, but no novice cohort has validated learning or transfer. The application includes a concrete observation/retest protocol.
- Advanced stages remain introductory. The 29-objective ledger explicitly lists missing practicals and incomplete subobjective coverage. This release does not fulfill comprehensive CCNA instruction.
- The two small pilot forms are not full-length certification mocks, are not calibrated for equal difficulty, and cannot predict a passing score. Some distractors are intentionally beginner-level; a deeper independently reviewed item bank is still needed.
- Packet Tracer instructions are build recipes, not binary starter files. They have not been executed against the user's installed Packet Tracer version; model/command differences and external completion are not automatically verified.
- Written explanations and portfolio evidence are not automatically graded. Rubric/mentor review remains necessary.
- Spaced prompts trigger after two days or an incorrect checkpoint; they are recommendations, not a validated retention algorithm.
- Browser storage remains local. The asynchronous adapter boundary and portable backups support future migration, but Firebase authentication, rules, conflict resolution and deployment are not configured.
- Browser-only assessment content can be inspected and local records can be changed. This is a personal learning tool, not a secure examination service.

## Verification

21 unit tests cover old/new migration, lab checks, malformed addresses, cross-octet subnet calculations, route selection, every injected model fault, form proportions, randomized answer mapping and pending-exam validation. Browser tests cover old and new flows at 1440, 390 and 320 pixels, save failures, progress retention, evidence, timed assessment resume/expiry and visible layout overflow. These establish software behavior, not educational efficacy.

## Remaining work toward the full recommendation set

Complete per-subobjective instructional depth and practicals, build a larger independently reviewed assessment bank, execute/version Packet Tracer starter projects, and conduct actual novice observation plus delayed transfer trials. Those remain visible gaps; the app does not label them complete.
